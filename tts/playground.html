Furby Pop & Haunted Voices

Every sound here started as plain text read by an AssemblyAI voice. Then we pitched, stacked, echoed and crushed it into a pop song and eight Halloween characters.

Track 01 · 128 BPM · Am F C G

I Am a Furby Girl

The Furby is the jane voice pitched up a few semitones with a light slapback echo. Her owner is george, pitched down slightly. The beat is synthesized in JavaScript and ducks under the vocals so every word stays clear.
